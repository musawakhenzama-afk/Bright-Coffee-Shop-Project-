**A BrightLearn Data Analytics Project**
**📘 Overview**
A complete data analysis case study for Bright Coffee Shop, created during the BrightLearn Data Analytics Bootcamp.
As a Junior Data Analyst, I transformed raw transactional sales data into clear insights to help the CEO improve revenue and product performance.
________________________________________
**🎯 Project Objectives**
•	Identify top revenue-generating products
•	Analyze sales trends across products and time periods
•	Determine the best-performing times of day
•	Provide actionable business recommendations
________________________________________

•	Top Revenue Drivers: e.g., Espresso, Latte, Cappuccino (insert your findings)
•	Peak Sales Hours: e.g., 8:00–10:30 AM, 4:00–6:00 PM

****🔍 Methodology**
**1. Data Planning & Architecture****
•	Created Data Flow Diagram (Miro)
•	Defined metrics:
o	Total Revenue = unit_price × transaction_qty
o	Units Sold (per product & category)
o	Sales by 30-minute time intervals
**2. Data Processing in Snowflake**
•	Imported raw CSV data
•	Cleaned inconsistent price formats (e.g., 3,1 → 3.1)
•	Added time-bucket column
•	Calculated revenue and summarized by product & time
**3. Data Analysis (Excel / Power BI)**
•	Built pivot tables and charts for:
o	Revenue by product
o	Time-interval sales
o	Best vs. worst performers
•	Added conditional formatting & interactive visuals
**4. Insights & Recommendations**
•	Interpreted sales patterns
•	Suggested inventory strategy, marketing ideas, and operational improvements
